## 2. The headline result

On a like-for-like basis the equity strategy trails index funds and does not beat
them on risk-adjusted return. Its real feature is a much shallower worst loss.

| 2012 → 2026-09, AUD | CAGR | Sharpe | Worst drawdown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy, pre-cash-credit (what the dashboard still shows) | 6.1% | 0.32 | −11% |
| Strategy at HEAD (idle cash now earns interest, commit `57676c4`) | 8.6% | 0.62 | −10% |
| The repo's benchmark — price indices, no dividends | 9.6% | 0.50 | −30% |
| A fair benchmark — index funds, dividends reinvested | 11.3% | 0.65 | −30% |

The cause is exposure, not signal: the book averages 34–44% invested. **All of
these numbers are survivorship-biased upper bounds** because no region has a
point-in-time membership file.

## 3. Three things verification overturned — do not act on the old versions

1. **The regime filter is NOT a drag.** With the gate off the drawdown breaker
   never fires (drawdown tops out at −22%, below the 25% trigger), so they are not
   duplicates; and on Calmar — return per unit of worst drawdown, the measure a
   crash filter exists to serve — the gate **wins in every sleeve** (US 0.73 vs
   0.53, ASX 0.71 vs 0.49). Replicated three times independently.
2. **Quarterly rebalancing is BETTER than monthly, and this is now the strongest
   unexploited lead in the equity book.** The earlier ablation used pandas' `QE`
   alias, which is one arbitrary quarterly phase and happens to be the worst of
   three. Averaged over phase: FTSE Sharpe 0.110 → 0.220 with cost drag cut from
   24.7% to ~11%; US 0.430 → 0.553; ASX 0.150 → 0.400; TSX 0.390 → 0.600. Annual
   beats quarterly in three of four sleeves, which no signal-decay story permits.
   This is [[Rebalance Timing Luck]] — the review fell into the trap it documents.
3. **The August phantom liquidation is fixed, regression-tested and already
   recorded** (`tests/test_paper_trade.py:367`;
   `docs/research/REALISED_TRADE_EVIDENCE.md:177`). Only the narration is missing.

One finding got **worse**: the neural agent voted on **274 of 281 trades** in
`matt` before 17 September, from model weights that no longer exist anywhere, with

## 4. LIVE AND URGENT

**Six currency crosses entered the live FX books on 22 September** and now carry
32.4% of `matt`'s gross risk, 34.1% of `partner`'s and 20.7% of `daytrader`'s
(EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY, AUDJPY, AUDNZD, EURAUD).

Cause: `forex/pairs.py` `DEFAULT_UNIVERSE` decides **both** what the neural model
trains on and what the books trade. The crosses were added for training rows;
`forex/fx_book.py:411-413` merges that list into every unlocked book on each run.
A cross is an arithmetic combination of two majors, so this is undisclosed
concentration on exposures the books already held, invisible to a per-instrument
position sizer.

The fix exists: commit **`f15a118`** on branch `feat/training-universe-split`
splits `TRAINING_UNIVERSE` (16, learned from) from `DEFAULT_UNIVERSE` (10,
traded). It is merged nowhere.

**Matt's decision: let the book restart handle the open positions** — do not
unwind them now. But `f15a118` **must land before the restart** (plan Task 23,
gating Task 26) or the reopened books come up on 16 symbols again.

## 7. What NOT to do

- Do not raise the vol target or add leverage before the exposure work lands.
- Do not lower `DSR_MIN`. The repo's own Monte Carlo study measured the
  alternatives disagreeing by a factor of forty and declined to switch.
- Do not build more ML on price-only daily inputs. Three independent instruments
  (a measured null, an empty champion roster, a permutation test at p = 0.4727)
  agree there is nothing there.
- Do not fold return-improving changes into the measurement block. That is the
  whole point of the block: if the numbers move for two reasons at once, neither
  can be attributed.

## 9. Traps worth not rediscovering

- **Every line number inherited from the review is stale.** HEAD moved during the
  work. Grep for the code.
- **`--synthetic` can clobber live books.** Always export `FX_STATE_DIR` and
  `MOMENTUM_STATE_DIR` to a scratch directory first.
- **A local test failure with CI green usually means a drifted venv**, not repo
  breakage. Check against `requirements.txt` floors first.
- **Run the full CI gate before pushing**, not just pytest: ruff, bandit,
  detect-secrets, then pytest.
- **The adversarial verifiers carry finding ids only in the workflow's return
  value**, not in the per-agent journal entries. Parse the task output file, not
  the journal, or ids collide.
